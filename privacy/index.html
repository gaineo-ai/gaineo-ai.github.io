Privacy policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

This policy explains how Gaineo ("Gaineo," "we," "us" or "our") collects, uses and shares personal information through our website at www.gaineo.ai, our sales and evaluation conversations, our events and the other ways we interact with you. It does not cover data that our customers process in the Gaineo platform, which our agreements with them govern, except as described in "Customer data in the Gaineo platform."

1. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy as our services change. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and change the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will take reasonable steps to let you know, for example by email if we have yours.

2. Personal information we collect

Information you give us

  • Get in touch form. Your first and last name, work email, company, and, if you choose to add them, your role, your revenue system of record and your message.
  • Email and conversations. What you send to info@gaineo.ai or share with us in calls and meetings.
  • Backtests and evaluations. If your company evaluates Gaineo, the business contact details of the people involved, and any data your company provides under a written agreement with us (see "Customer data in the Gaineo platform" below).
  • Events. Contact details you share with us at conferences, meetups and other events.
  • Job applications. The information in your application, such as your contact details and resume.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs. Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which records standard technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request.
  • Fonts. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser shares its IP address and browser details with Google when a page loads.
  • Usage and device information. We use HubSpot analytics to understand how visitors find and use our website, such as the pages you view, how long you stay, the site that referred you, your browser and device type, and your approximate location derived from your IP address.

Cookies and similar technologies

HubSpot sets analytics cookies, which are small text files stored in your browser, on our behalf. They recognize repeat visits and measure how the site is used. The main ones are hubspotutk and __hstc, which identify your browser and last six months, __hssc and __hssrc, which track the current session, and __hs_opt_out and __hs_cookie_cat_pref, which remember your cookie choice. We use what they collect to improve our website and content. If you submit our Get in touch form, HubSpot links the pages you have viewed from that browser, before and after, to your contact record, so we can understand what you are interested in.

We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels, we do not use analytics data to show you ads on other sites, and we have turned off HubSpot's features that share visitor data for its own purposes. Visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before these cookies are set. Visitors elsewhere see a notice. See "Your choices and rights" for how to change your choice.

Information from other sources

We may receive business contact information from publicly available professional sources such as LinkedIn, from people who introduce us, and from partners and event organizers.

3. How we use personal information

  • To respond to your inquiry and arrange conversations, demos and backtests.
  • To evaluate whether Gaineo is a fit for your company and to provide the services you or your company ask for.
  • To send you updates about Gaineo that we think are relevant. You can opt out at any time.
  • To operate, maintain and secure our website and services, and to prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To understand how our website is used and to improve it and our content.
  • To review job applications.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.
  • To create de-identified or aggregated information that does not identify you.

4. Customer data in the Gaineo platform

When a company uses the Gaineo platform, including during a trial or backtest, it may provide revenue data from systems such as its CRM and CPQ tools. That data can include information about the company's employees, customers and prospects. We process it on the company's behalf, as its service provider, under our agreement with that company, and that agreement governs how the data is handled. If you have questions about data a company has shared with us, please contact that company first.

We use a customer's data only to provide and support the services for that customer. Models trained on a customer's data serve that customer only. We do not use one customer's data to train models for other customers, and we do not sell customer data.

5. How we share personal information

We share personal information only as described below.

  • Service providers that work on our behalf under contracts that limit how they may use the information, including HubSpot (customer relationship management, the Get in touch form and website analytics), GitHub (website hosting), Google (web fonts, email and productivity tools), and the cloud and AI providers that run the Gaineo platform.
  • Professional advisers such as lawyers, accountants and auditors.
  • Legal and safety. When we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by law or legal process, or is needed to protect the rights, property or safety of Gaineo, our customers or others.
  • Business transfers. In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of assets, subject to standard confidentiality protections.
  • With your direction or consent.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Marketing email. Use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email, or write to us, and we will stop sending them. We may still send messages about services you use.
  • Access, correction and deletion. You can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to info@gaineo.ai. We will verify the request and respond within the time the law requires, and in any case within 45 days.
  • California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose, to request its deletion or correction, and to opt out of its sale or sharing (we do not sell or share it). We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. You may use an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf.
  • People in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland may also object to or restrict certain processing, and may request a portable copy of their information. We rely on our legitimate interests in responding to inquiries and growing our business, on performing a contract, on legal obligations and, where needed, on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.
  • Cookies. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, use the Cookie settings link at the bottom of any page to change your choice at any time. Wherever you are, you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or send a Global Privacy Control signal. Our website works without analytics cookies. Choices apply per browser and per device.
  • Browser signals. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we automatically decline analytics cookies for you, and we treat the signal as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information in any case. We do not respond to Do Not Track signals, because there is no common standard for them.

7. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes above. Inquiry and contact records are kept while there is an active or potential business relationship, and we delete or de-identify records that have had no activity for 24 months. Analytics cookies expire within six months. Browsing history linked to a contact record is kept with that record, and aggregate traffic reports that do not identify you may be kept longer. Customer data in the Gaineo platform is kept and deleted as set out in the customer's agreement. We may keep information longer where the law requires it.

8. Security

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information, including encryption in transit and access limited to the people who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International transfers

Gaineo is based in the United States, and we and our service providers store and process information in the United States. If you are outside the United States, your information will be transferred there, where data protection laws may differ from those where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, for these transfers.

10. Children

Our website and services are for businesses and are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or want to make a request, write to info@gaineo.ai or to Gaineo, 548 Market St, San Francisco, CA 94104.
