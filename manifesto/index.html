Manifesto

AI can do more than save time.

01 · The discipline, plus AI

A proven discipline, now with AI.

Some of the most competitive industries in the world learned long ago that revenue is managed, not hoped for. Airlines, hotels, cruise lines and car rental firms sell perishable capacity in markets where a rival can match a price within minutes. They answered with discipline: segmenting customers precisely, pricing each segment to its value and timing, and allocating capacity to where it earns the most.

That discipline has let them defend revenue and margin in some of the most price-competitive markets anywhere. We believe B2B revenue is ready for the same rigor, and that AI now makes it possible to bring it to the complexity of enterprise deals.

Discipline is how competitive industries defend revenue.
B2B is next.

02 · Connected, the data pays

Connect the revenue lifecycle, and the hidden gains surface.

Every system in a revenue stack keeps a faithful record of its part: the opportunity, the quote, the order, the recognized revenue, the cost. Together they preserve the operational truth of a business, as they should.

We believe the most valuable questions sit between them. What mix of price, terms and priority best serves the quarter? Where can margin improve without costing the win? How should scarce capacity be spread across customers and regions? Answering them means understanding how decisions, constraints and outcomes relate across the whole lifecycle. That is where hidden efficiency lives, and where functions find the alignment that reaches the bottom line.

Systems of record tell you where you stand.
Connected, they show your best next move.

03 · Optimization, not just automation

The true potential of AI is optimization, not just task automation.

Generative AI has made information more accessible than ever, and automating routine work is a real advance. Yet an eloquent answer is not the same as a better decision. The firms that lead capital markets do not build their advantage on recording trades. Their quants model probabilities, simulate outcomes and optimize continuously, within acceptable constraints.

We believe every company deserves that kind of quantitative intelligence, built on a disciplined approach and a connected view of its revenue. Every approval, override and outcome becomes part of what the company knows, so each quarter's decisions sharpen the next. That learning compounds into an asset the company owns, and an advantage that widens for those who start early.

Automation saves time.
Optimization compounds.
