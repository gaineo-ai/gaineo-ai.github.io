Our journey

The road to grounded revenue intelligence.

We wrestled with many notions of what AI could do for revenue teams, and settled on the one that ultimately matters: AI operationalized to produce better revenue outcomes, beyond productivity gains.

Model, then orchestrate. We built grounded intelligence models first, then an orchestration layer that brings that intelligence to every persona. It does more than ease the human relay: it shows real pathways forward at the point of every decision.
